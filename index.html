01 / About

About me.

I am a first-year Economics, Finance and Data Science student at Imperial College London, having graduated from Methodist College Kuala Lumpur with four A*s in Economics, Mathematics, Further Mathematics and Physics. Alongside my degree, I work with Dr Elizabeth Lampe at Dartmouth College's Center for Technology and Behavioral Health on two co-authored research papers. My work includes reproducing statistical analyses, testing assumptions and writing manuscript sections. Our first paper is now available as a PsyArXiv preprint, while the second is in preparation. Previously, I completed a research internship with Dr Kelly Zhang in Imperial's Department of Mathematics, reviewing reinforcement-learning methods for digital health. I am interested in quantitative trading, financial markets and applied AI, and I am currently completing Career26 Academy's Quant Trading programme. Teaching, community initiatives and team projects remain an important part of what I do.
