RiverLens Arena

Independent poker AI research platform
Aug 2026 - now

Built an end-to-end six-player Hold'em research platform with 3.94M+ Monte Carlo completions, a custom 14,465-parameter neural equity model and a checkpointed external-sampling MCCFR lab. I generated a reproducible 50,000-state dataset, ran 200,000 MCCFR iterations across four workers and evaluated strategies with paired confidence intervals, calibration analysis and a locked baseline.
